Grow your Marvel

The same 303 characters from Week 1's real Wikipedia network, wired up all over again — one arrival at a time, under preferential attachment (the Barabási–Albert growth model). Characters join in random order and link to whoever already has the most connections. No editors, no citations — just chance and the rich-get-richer rule.

Toggle the growth on, and watch a brand new alternate universe assemble. When a character becomes a statistically significant hub here — or two characters who barely know each other in the real network suddenly get wired directly together — you'll get a pop-up with a mock comic cover for the moment. The side panel always shows the biggest hubs in both universes, real and alternate, so you can compare.
