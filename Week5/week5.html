Surprises

Three things we didn't expect

2 / 60

Its only misses had broken prompts

The counter lost just two pairs. They are exactly the two heroes whose description in the course data is cut off mid-sentence.

33 words

It didn't just copy Wikipedia

Its longest copied stretch is the stock opening line, "appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics…", which real articles share too.

2×

Famous heroes look fake

Real articles about famous heroes look twice as "AI" to the counter as obscure ones: long sentences, lots of brackets.

How we did it

Three steps

Pick 60 heroes

From the course's Marvel network: 20 famous, 20 in between, 20 obscure.

Let an AI forge them

Each article came from its own Claude Fable 5.1 agent, given only the name, a one-line description and the linked heroes. It never saw the real article, and we checked every agent's log.

Count words

Twelve simple counts (sentence length, punctuation, rare word pairs…) feed a basic statistical model.

Fine print. You read 120 words; the counter reads the whole article (up to 1,500), which makes its job easier. The agents ran inside Claude Code, and we ran only round 1 of the planned arms race.
