What surprised us

  • 2 of 60. The counter lost only two pairs, and they are exactly the two heroes whose description in the course data is cut off mid-sentence.
  • 33 words. The longest stretch Fable copied from Wikipedia is the stock opening line, “appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics…”, which real articles share too.
  • 2×. Real articles about famous heroes look twice as suspicious to the counter as articles about obscure ones. They have the long sentences and brackets Fable likes.

How we did it

  1. Picked 60 heroes from the course's Marvel network: 20 famous, 20 in between, 20 obscure.
  2. Gave each one to its own Claude Fable 5.1 agent with only the name, a one-line description and the linked heroes. No agent saw the real article, and we checked every agent's log.
  3. Scored every article on 12 simple counts (sentence length, punctuation, rare word pairs) with a textbook statistical model.

You read 120 words, while the counter reads the whole article (up to 1,500), which makes its job easier. The agents ran inside Claude Code, and only round 1 of the planned arms race was run. More: full write-up · analysis notebook · how we used AI.
