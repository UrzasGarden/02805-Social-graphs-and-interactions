Geheneris Hala'son Mahr Vehl, usually shortened to Mahr Vehl, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Warren Ellis and artist Steve McNiven, he first appeared in Ultimate Secret #1 (May 2005). He is the Ultimate Marvel counterpart of Mar-Vell, the original Captain Marvel, and like him he lives on Earth under the assumed identity of a human scientist named Lawson.

A Kree officer who defects to warn humanity of the approaching world-destroyer Gah Lak Tus, he serves as an ally of S.H.I.E.L.D., the Ultimates and the Fantastic Four in the Ultimate Galactus Trilogy, and was later killed in Ultimate Comics: Ultimates.

== Publication history ==

Mahr Vehl was introduced in Ultimate Secret, a four-issue limited series written by Warren Ellis and drawn by Steve McNiven and Tom Raney, published in 2005. The series formed the middle part of the Ultimate Galactus Trilogy, between Ultimate Nightmare (2004) and Ultimate Extinction (2006). The character was Ellis's reinterpretation of Mar-Vell, created by Stan Lee and Gene Colan in 1967; his alias Philip Lawson echoes the Walter Lawson identity of the original character, whose Captain Marvel name later passed to his son Genis-Vell in the mainstream Marvel Universe.

Mahr Vehl returned in Ultimate Extinction, a five-issue series by Ellis and artist Brandon Peterson that concluded the trilogy in 2006. After several years without appearances, he was used by writer Jonathan Hickman and artist Esad Ribić in Ultimate Comics: Ultimates in 2011–2012, where the character was killed off.

== Fictional character biography ==

Mahr Vehl is an officer of the Kree Void Navy assigned to a stealth vessel observing Earth. The Kree monitor the planet because it lies in the path of Gah Lak Tus, a vast swarm of machine intelligences that sterilizes inhabited worlds, and they intend to study its approach rather than intervene. Unwilling to let humanity be destroyed, Mahr Vehl has his body surgically altered and fitted with cybernetic implants so that he can pass as human.

Under the name Dr. Philip Lawson he joins a NASA project developing the Asis, an experimental spacecraft, and quietly pushes the work toward technology that might let humans escape or defend their world. His cover is exposed when his superiors learn of his defection and send a Kree kill team after him. Mahr Vehl reveals his armored Kree form to repel the attackers in front of S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Carol Danvers and is taken into custody by Nick Fury.

Interrogated at the Triskelion, he explains the nature of Gah Lak Tus and the Kree policy of non-interference. Fury assigns him to work with the Ultimates and the Fantastic Four, and Mahr Vehl guides a joint team aboard the Asis to the Kree ship, where they fight its commander, Yahn Rgg, and seize the Kree records on the swarm.

In Ultimate Extinction, Mahr Vehl works alongside Reed Richards as the swarm nears Earth and its silver-skinned heralds spread a suicide cult among the population. He takes part in the defense when the first Gah Lak Tus modules arrive, and the swarm is finally repelled by a telepathic assault organized by Charles Xavier combined with a device built by Richards that releases the energy of a newborn universe.

Mahr Vehl resurfaces in Ultimate Comics: Ultimates as an adviser to Fury. When the Children of Tomorrow, a post-human society created by a now hostile Reed Richards, emerge from their domed City in Germany, Mahr Vehl joins the Ultimates and the European Defense Initiative in an assault on the City and is killed in the fighting.

== Powers and abilities ==

As a Kree, Mahr Vehl is stronger and more durable than a human, and his body has been reinforced with cybernetic implants that support his surgically created human disguise. He wears Kree battle armor that allows flight, including travel through space, and incorporates energy weapons, force fields and sensors. He is a trained naval officer and an accomplished engineer with a detailed knowledge of Kree technology.
