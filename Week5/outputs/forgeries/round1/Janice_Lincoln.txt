Janice Lincoln is a supervillain appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Ed Brubaker and artist Mitch Breitweiser, the character first appeared in Captain America #607 (August 2010). She is the daughter of the crime lord Lonnie Lincoln (Tombstone) and the fourth character to use the Beetle identity, following Abner Jenkins, Leila Davis and an unnamed operative of the Thunderbolts. A trained lawyer who deliberately chose a career in costumed crime, she has been a member of Boomerang's version of the Sinister Six and the leader of the Syndicate, and is a recurring opponent of Spider-Man.

== Publication history ==

Janice Lincoln debuted as the new Beetle in Captain America #607 (August 2010), in the storyline "No Escape" by Ed Brubaker and Mitch Breitweiser, where she was introduced as a hired operative working for Baron Zemo against Bucky Barnes. Her background and family connection to Tombstone were not revealed at the time.

The character was developed substantially in The Superior Foes of Spider-Man (2013–2015) by writer Nick Spencer and artist Steve Lieber, a comedic series that followed a small-time incarnation of the Sinister Six. The series established her as Tombstone's daughter and gave her an origin story, and the title's critical success raised her profile considerably. Spencer later used the character again in The Amazing Spider-Man (vol. 5) beginning in 2018, where she assembled an all-female criminal team, the Syndicate, and resumed a romantic relationship with Boomerang.

Lincoln appeared in the 2023–2024 crossover "Gang War", which ran through The Amazing Spider-Man and several tie-in miniseries including Luke Cage: Gang War, in which she attempted to secure her father's criminal territory.

== Fictional character biography ==

Janice Lincoln is the daughter of Lonnie Lincoln, the albino gangster known as Tombstone. Her father, wanting her to have a legitimate life, pays for her education and she becomes an attorney. Janice, however, grows up idolizing her father's lifestyle and decides that she wants to be a criminal in her own right. Tombstone reluctantly arranges for her to receive a suit of Beetle armor through Baron Zemo and the Fixer, who equip her as part of a scheme of their own.

In her first outing, Zemo and the Fixer send Janice to attack Bucky Barnes, who is serving as Captain America at the time. She confronts Barnes and Black Widow (Natasha Romanova) in a public setting, but she is inexperienced and is quickly defeated and taken into custody. Zemo's plot is later revealed to have been a distraction intended to discredit Barnes by exposing his past as the Winter Soldier.

After her release, Janice joins Boomerang's Sinister Six alongside Shocker, Speed Demon and Overdrive. The team is far from the top tier of New York's criminal underworld, and much of their time is spent squabbling, running petty scams and avoiding the attention of the Chameleon and the Owl. Janice is frequently the most pragmatic member of the group and openly disdains Boomerang's leadership. When the team learns that Boomerang has been planning to betray them to the authorities, the others turn on him, and Janice briefly attempts to take control of the operation herself. The group's hunt for a painting supposedly depicting the true face of Victor von Doom ends with everyone involved cheated of their prize.

Later, Janice forms the Syndicate, a team made up of female villains including Lady Octopus, Scorpia, White Rabbit, Electro (Francine Frye) and Trapstr. The group initially hires itself out and comes into conflict with Spider-Man and with Boomerang, who has by this point become a reluctant roommate of Peter Parker. Despite their public antagonism, Janice and Boomerang rekindle their relationship, and she is shown to genuinely care for him even as she dismisses his incompetence. The Syndicate is later used by the Kingpin as part of a broader effort to control the city's costumed criminals.

During "Gang War", Tombstone is gravely injured following an attack at the wedding of his associate Randy Robertson, and a power vacuum develops in New York's underworld as crime families fight over territory. Janice moves to protect her father's holdings and asserts herself as a force in her own right, placing her in opposition to Spider-Man, the Mayor Luke Cage and Iron Fist (Danny Rand), who are attempting to end the violence. Her father recovers and the two share an uneasy respect, with Tombstone acknowledging that his daughter has become the criminal he never wanted her to be.

== Powers and abilities ==

Janice Lincoln has no superhuman abilities of her own. As the Beetle she wears a suit of powered armor originally supplied by the Fixer, which grants her enhanced strength and durability, flight by means of insectoid wings, and electrical blasts fired from gauntlets or from the armor's mandibles. The armor is also fitted with adhesive pads for climbing surfaces. Later versions of the suit, upgraded during her time with the Syndicate, are more streamlined and include improved targeting and defensive systems.

She is a qualified lawyer and has occasionally used her legal training to her advantage, both in negotiating on behalf of herself and her teammates and in exploiting loopholes in the criminal justice system. She is a capable strategist and manipulator, though her combat experience is limited compared to many of her opponents.

== Reception ==

Janice Lincoln's portrayal in The Superior Foes of Spider-Man was generally well received by critics, who praised the series for its characterization of its criminal cast and singled out the Beetle as one of its strongest members. In 2020, CBR.com ranked her among the most notable characters to have used the Beetle identity, and the same site included her in a list of Spider-Man villains who deserved more attention. Reviewers of The Amazing Spider-Man (vol. 5) noted the relationship between Lincoln and Boomerang as a highlight of the series' lighter issues.
