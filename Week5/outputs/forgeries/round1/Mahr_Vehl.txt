Geheneris Hala'son Mahr Vehl, commonly known as Mahr Vehl, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Warren Ellis and artist Steve McNiven, the character first appeared in Ultimate Secret #1 (May 2005). He is the Ultimate Marvel counterpart of Mar-Vell, the original Captain Marvel, and like that character he is a Kree officer who adopts a human identity, in this case that of the scientist Philip Lawson, while stationed on Earth.

== Publication history ==

Mahr Vehl was introduced in the four-issue limited series Ultimate Secret (2005), the second chapter of Warren Ellis's loosely connected "Ultimate Galactus" trilogy, which also included Ultimate Nightmare (2004) and Ultimate Extinction (2006). McNiven drew the first two issues of Ultimate Secret and Tom Raney completed the series. Ellis reworked several elements of the mainstream Mar-Vell, including the Lawson alias and the character's divided loyalty between the Kree Empire and humanity, while discarding others; the Ultimate version has no equivalent of Rick Jones and no son corresponding to Genis-Vell.

The character returned in Ultimate Extinction (2006), illustrated by Brandon Peterson, in which the threat foreshadowed in the earlier series arrives on Earth. After a period of absence, Mahr Vehl was brought back by writer Jonathan Hickman in the relaunched Ultimate Comics: Ultimates in 2011, where he was used as a supporting member of S.H.I.E.L.D.'s forces and written out early in the run.

== Fictional character biography ==

Mahr Vehl is a captain in the Kree military who is assigned to observe Earth, which the Kree have marked for destruction by the vast machine intelligence Gah Lak Tus. Kree protocol forbids interference, but Mahr Vehl grows sympathetic to the humans he studies. Surgically altered to pass as a human being, he takes the name Dr. Philip Lawson and joins the staff of a NASA project building a faster-than-light spacecraft. His intent is to help humanity leave the planet before Gah Lak Tus arrives.

When the project is attacked by Kree agents who have learned of his defection, Lawson reveals his true form and fights alongside the Ultimates and the Fantastic Four. Taken into custody by S.H.I.E.L.D., he explains to Nick Fury that the Kree have positioned a fleet to witness Earth's extinction and that Gah Lak Tus has consumed countless inhabited worlds. Using his ship and knowledge of Kree communications, he helps Earth's heroes locate the fleet, and the Ultimates seize its records of the approaching entity. He remains on Earth afterward as an adviser to Fury.

In the storyline "Ultimate Extinction", Mahr Vehl works with Reed Richards and Fury as Gah Lak Tus reaches the solar system. He provides Kree intelligence about the entity's structure and participates in the final assault, during which Richards uses Kree-derived technology to drive Gah Lak Tus away from Earth.

Years later, in Ultimate Comics: Ultimates, Mahr Vehl is operating as a S.H.I.E.L.D. asset when the Children of Tomorrow, the enhanced population created by Richards in the Dome in Germany, declare war on the outside world. Fury dispatches him with a strike force into the Children's territory, where he is overwhelmed and killed, an early demonstration of how far the Children's technology has outstripped that of both Earth and the Kree.

== Powers and abilities ==

Mahr Vehl is a member of the Kree race and possesses physical strength, durability and reflexes somewhat greater than those of a human. His body was altered by Kree surgeons to appear human, and he can shift between his human guise and his natural blue-skinned form. He is a trained soldier and starship pilot with extensive knowledge of Kree science and military procedure.

Most of his abilities come from his Kree battle armor, a cybernetic system bonded to his nervous system. The armor allows him to fly, survive in space, project force fields and fire energy blasts from his gauntlets. It can also interface with and override other Kree technology, which enables him to break into the communications of the Kree fleet. Without the armor his offensive capabilities are limited.
