Gauntlet (Joseph Green) is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character was created by writer Dan Slott and artist Stefano Caselli and first appeared in Avengers: The Initiative #1 (June 2007). A career United States Army sergeant, Green draws his powers from an alien weapon bonded to his right arm and served as the drill instructor at Camp Hammond, the training facility of the Fifty State Initiative.

== Publication history ==

Gauntlet was introduced as a supporting character in Avengers: The Initiative, the ongoing series launched in the aftermath of the "Civil War" storyline to follow the government's program for registered superhumans. Slott and co-writer Christos Gage used the character as a foil for the young recruits, and he appeared regularly throughout the title's 35-issue run from 2007 to 2010, including its tie-ins to "World War Hulk", "Secret Invasion", "Dark Reign" and "Siege". He later made minor appearances in Avengers Academy and other Marvel titles.

== Fictional character biography ==

Joseph Green is a soldier serving in Afghanistan when his unit uncovers a damaged alien weapon in the form of a large metal gauntlet. The device fuses to his arm and cannot be removed. Rather than discharging him, the military assigns Green to Camp Hammond in Stamford, Connecticut, where he becomes the senior drill instructor under Henry Peter Gyrich, working alongside instructors such as Hank Pym, Justice and She-Hulk. Green is a harsh disciplinarian who frequently mocks the recruits, and in particular the former New Warriors, whom he blames for the Stamford disaster.

During the "World War Hulk" storyline, Green leads the trainees against the Hulk's Warbound in New York City, where several recruits are badly hurt. Shortly afterwards, Green is found beaten nearly to death on the grounds of the camp. The assault is initially blamed on the New Warriors, but it is eventually revealed that the attacker was the recruit Slapstick, who was angered by Green's insults toward his former team. Green recovers after his gauntlet begins to repair his body of its own accord.

The gauntlet is subsequently shown to be a fragment of the same alien technology as the Tactigon, the weapon that empowers the clone KIA. When KIA attacks Camp Hammond, the gauntlet asserts a will of its own and briefly takes control of Green in an effort to reunite with the other weapon. Green regains control and helps defeat KIA. In the "Secret Invasion" storyline, he fights alongside the Initiative and the Skrull Kill Krew against the invading Skrulls after Pym is exposed as a Skrull impostor.

When Norman Osborn assumes control of the Initiative during "Dark Reign" and installs the Hood and Taskmaster as its leaders, Green refuses to serve under criminals. He is court-martialed and the gauntlet is forcibly removed from his arm. Green deserts and joins the Avengers Resistance, the underground group formed by Justice and Tigra, and takes part in the assault on Camp H.A.M.M.E.R. during the "Siege" storyline. After Osborn's fall, Steve Rogers restores the gauntlet to Green and places him in charge of training the new Avengers Academy.

== Powers and abilities ==

Green possesses no innate superhuman powers. His abilities derive entirely from the alien gauntlet, which is grafted to his right forearm. The device can project concussive energy blasts and generate solid constructs, most often in the form of an enormous energy hand that can strike, grasp or shield. The gauntlet is capable of self-repair and can heal its wearer, and it has demonstrated a limited intelligence that sometimes acts against Green's wishes. Green is also an experienced soldier and a skilled hand-to-hand combatant and firearms instructor.
