The Union is a fictional superhero team appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Paul Grist and artist Andrea Di Vito, the team first appeared in The Union #1 (February 2021). The Union is a government-sponsored team based in the United Kingdom, with each of its members intended to represent one of the constituent countries of the UK. Its founding roster consists of Britannia, Union Jack (Joseph Chapman), Snakes, Kelpie and the Choir.

The team was introduced as a tie-in to the "King in Black" crossover and its debut miniseries dealt with the group being assembled for political reasons shortly before an invasion of Earth by the symbiote god Knull.

== Publication history ==

The Union was announced by Marvel Comics in early 2020 as a five-issue limited series written by Paul Grist, a British creator known for his independent titles Jack Staff and Kane, with art by Andrea Di Vito. The series was originally scheduled to launch in the spring of 2020 as part of the "Empyre" event, but its release was postponed when the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted comics distribution. When the book returned to Marvel's schedule it had been reworked to tie into "King in Black" instead, and the first issue was released in December 2020 with a cover date of February 2021.

Grist described the premise as a deliberately topical one, with the team formed as a public relations exercise in the wake of the United Kingdom's departure from the European Union. The series ran for five issues, concluding in 2021, and was later collected in trade paperback. The characters have made only occasional appearances since the miniseries ended, with Union Jack continuing to appear elsewhere as an established Marvel character.

== Fictional character biography ==

The Union is created at the direction of Steve Darwin, a civil servant attached to the British government, who wants a superhero team that can serve as a unifying national symbol following Brexit. The team is built around Britannia, a newly introduced hero presented to the press as its leader and public face. Alongside her, Darwin recruits the Choir, a Welsh woman whose voice carries supernatural power; Kelpie, a Scottish hero connected to the water spirits of folklore; and Snakes, a young man from Northern Ireland who can transform his body into a mass of serpents. The experienced Union Jack (Joseph Chapman) is added to the roster for England, although he is openly sceptical of the team's purpose and resents being used as a prop.

The team's first public appearance in London is interrupted when the forces of Knull reach Earth during the storyline "King in Black". Dragon-like symbiotes attack the city, and Britannia is killed while trying to hold them off. With the team's figurehead dead on its first day, Union Jack reluctantly takes command of the survivors, despite having no interest in leadership.

In the aftermath, the remaining members struggle to work together. The Choir, Kelpie and Snakes are inexperienced and distrust one another, while Union Jack suspects that Darwin has not been truthful about why the team was formed. The group comes into conflict with Doc Croc, a crocodile-like criminal operating beneath London, and in the course of the investigation discovers that Britannia's origin and recruitment were not what the public had been told. The series ends with Union Jack and the others deciding to continue as the Union on their own terms, independent of the ministers and officials who had created them.

== Membership ==

Britannia is the team's original leader, a heavily armoured and super-strong hero introduced specifically for the series and killed in its first issue. Union Jack (Joseph Chapman) is the third person to use the Union Jack identity, a Marvel Comics mantle dating back to the Invaders stories of the 1970s; he is a working-class former art student from Manchester with peak human conditioning and extensive combat training, and he becomes the team's field leader. The Choir, representing Wales, generates sonic effects through song. Kelpie, representing Scotland, is tied to the shape-shifting water horses of Scottish legend and is able to manipulate water. Snakes, representing Northern Ireland, can dissolve his body into a swarm of snakes and reform at will.

== Powers and abilities ==

The members of the Union have a range of abilities drawn largely from British and Irish folklore. Britannia possesses superhuman strength and durability and carries a shield and trident, in keeping with the national personification she is named after. The Choir's voice can project damaging sound waves and can be used to disorient or calm opponents. Kelpie can control water and assume a liquid or equine form. Snakes can split into dozens of serpents, allowing him to slip through small spaces and reconstitute himself elsewhere. Union Jack has no superhuman powers but is a highly trained fighter, armed with a revolver, a dagger and a variety of equipment supplied by British intelligence.

== Reception ==

The Union received a mixed to moderately positive critical response. Reviewers generally praised Grist's characterisation of Union Jack and the series' willingness to engage directly with British politics, while some criticised the decision to kill off the heavily promoted Britannia in the opening issue and felt that the new characters were given too little room to develop over five issues. The book's connection to "King in Black" was also noted as a mixed blessing, bringing attention to the title while constraining its story. Several commentators placed the series in a tradition of Marvel UK and Captain Britain-related titles that have attempted to establish a distinctly British corner of the Marvel Universe.

== In other media ==

The Union has not appeared outside comics. Union Jack, the team's most prominent member, has appeared independently in animated series, video games and merchandise associated with Captain America and the Invaders.
