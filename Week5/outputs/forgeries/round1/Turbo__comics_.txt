Turbo is the name of two superheroes appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Both characters, Michiko "Mickey" Musashi and Michael "Mike" Jeffries, were created by writer Fabian Nicieza and artist Darick Robertson and first appeared in New Warriors #28 (October 1992). The two college students share a suit of flight armor once worn by the hero Torpedo, and Musashi eventually becomes its sole user and a longtime member of the New Warriors. She later appears as a member of the support group Excelsior, also known as the Loners.

== Publication history ==

The Turbo armor predates the characters who wear it. It was introduced as the costume of the Torpedo in Daredevil #126 (October 1975) by Marv Wolfman and Sal Buscema, where it was worn first by its designer Michael Stivak and then by former football player Brock Jones. Jones became a supporting character in Rom the Space Knight, in which Bill Mantlo established that Stivak's uncle, Senator Eugene Stivak, was secretly a Dire Wraith, which tied the suit's technology to that alien race. Brock Jones was killed in the pages of Rom while defending the town of Clairton, West Virginia.

Fabian Nicieza and Darick Robertson revived the armor for New Warriors #28 (October 1992), where it was found by two students at Empire State University. Nicieza divided the identity between Mike Jeffries, who located the suit, and Mickey Musashi, who proved to be the more capable pilot. Musashi became a regular member of the team and remained part of the cast through the end of the first volume with New Warriors #75 (September 1996). The storyline involving the Dire Wraith queen Volx in New Warriors #71–75 (1996) resolved the suit's alien origins and ended Jeffries' tenure.

Musashi returned as a core member of New Warriors vol. 2 (1999–2000) by Jay Faerber and Steve Scott, which ran for ten issues. After several years of sporadic appearances, Brian K. Vaughan and Adrian Alphona used her in Runaways vol. 2 #1–6 (2005) as a member of Excelsior, a support group for former teenage superheroes organized by Phil Urich. That group received its own six-issue limited series, The Loners (2007), by C. B. Cebulski and Karl Moline, in which Musashi was one of the lead characters. She has since made guest appearances in titles connected to the New Warriors and the Loners.

== Fictional character biography ==

== Mike Jeffries and Mickey Musashi ==

Michael Jeffries is a student at Empire State University in New York City and an admirer of the Torpedo, a short-lived hero of the 1970s. He discovers the Torpedo's armor, which had been left behind after Brock Jones's death, and brings it to his friend and fellow student Michiko Musashi. Jeffries, a physical education major, assumes he will be the natural wearer of the suit, but Musashi quickly demonstrates a better grasp of its controls and more composure in the air. The two agree to share the costume and the name Turbo, taking turns in the field without revealing to anyone that two people are using the same identity.

Turbo first encounters the New Warriors when the team's activities draw the attention of the press and of Musashi, who is pursuing a journalism degree. After helping the team during a crisis in Manhattan, Musashi is invited to join on a provisional basis by Night Thrasher, and she becomes a full member alongside Hindsight Lad and Rage. Nova (Richard Rider) in particular treats her as a peer, and the two develop a working friendship that lasts through their later service together. Jeffries continues to use the suit on occasion, but his contributions go largely unnoticed by the team, which assumes that Musashi is the only Turbo.

The arrangement becomes a source of growing resentment. Jeffries feels that Musashi has claimed the identity he discovered, while Musashi finds his impulsiveness dangerous and begins to keep the suit for herself. Their friendship frays as the New Warriors face a series of threats, including the time-displaced Sphinx and the villain group Psionex, and Musashi's standing on the team continues to rise.

== Volx and the death of Mike Jeffries ==

The origin of the armor returns to haunt its wearers when Volx, the queen of the Dire Wraiths, arrives on Earth. Nearly all of her race had been banished to Limbo by Rom, and Volx intends to use the Torpedo suit, which was built from Wraith technology, to power a device capable of neutralizing superhuman abilities worldwide. She tracks the suit to New York and takes Jeffries by surprise while he is wearing it. Volx kills Jeffries and claims the armor, assuming the Turbo identity herself in order to infiltrate the New Warriors.

Musashi, grieving and furious, works with the New Warriors to expose the impostor. The team is joined by other heroes who had history with the Dire Wraiths, and in the ensuing battle Volx's plan is defeated and she is killed. Musashi recovers the armor, now the only Turbo, and resolves to continue in Jeffries' memory. She remains with the New Warriors until the team disbands following the dissolution of its original roster.

== Later career ==

When Nova and Speedball reform the New Warriors, Musashi rejoins them along with Namorita and new recruits Bolt and Aegis. During this period the team operates out of a brownstone in Brooklyn and faces enemies such as Blastaar and a reorganized Psionex. Musashi balances her superhero activities with her studies and increasingly questions whether she wants to spend her adult life in costume. She eventually leaves the group to focus on a career in journalism.

Some years later, Musashi is approached by Phil Urich, formerly the heroic Green Goblin, who is forming Excelsior, a Los Angeles support group for young people who want to leave superhero life behind. The group, which includes Darkhawk, Lightspeed, Ricochet and Chamber, is secretly funded by Rick Jones and attempts to persuade the Runaways to give up their activities. In The Loners, the group continues to meet while its members struggle to honor their pledge not to use their powers. Musashi is drawn back into action when Fujikawa Industries attempts to seize the Torpedo armor, and the series ends with the group fractured after Urich steals Darkhawk's amulet. Musashi grows close to Ricochet during this period and is among the members who choose to stay together.

== Powers and abilities ==

Neither Musashi nor Jeffries possesses superhuman abilities of their own; all of Turbo's powers derive from the Torpedo armor. The suit is equipped with turbine units mounted on the forearms and lower legs that allow the wearer to fly at speeds in excess of 500 miles per hour and to maneuver with considerable precision. The same turbines can be used to project concentrated blasts of air powerful enough to knock an opponent off their feet or shatter concrete, and the wearer can generate a vortex by spinning rapidly in flight.

The armor itself is bulletproof and insulates the wearer against the friction and cold encountered at high altitude. Because it was constructed from Dire Wraith technology, the suit also contains systems that its original human users never fully understood, and it has shown a degree of responsiveness to the wearer's thoughts. Musashi is a skilled pilot and a trained journalist, and during her time with the New Warriors she received instruction in hand-to-hand combat.

== Reception ==

Turbo has generally been regarded as a minor member of the New Warriors. In 2017, Comic Book Resources included Musashi in a list of New Warriors members it considered deserving of greater attention, noting that her role in the team's final storyline was largely overlooked. Her appearance in The Loners was cited by reviewers as one of the more developed portrayals of a former teenage hero adjusting to ordinary life.
