Rockslide (Santo Vaccarro) is a fictional mutant superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writers Nunzio DeFilippis and Christina Weir and artist Keron Grant, the character first appeared in New Mutants vol. 2 #7 (December 2003). Santo is an Italian-American teenager from Boston whose body is composed of living rock. He was introduced as a student at the Xavier Institute, where he was assigned to the Hellions training squad overseen by Emma Frost alongside Hellion, Mercury, Tag, Wither and Dust, and he later served with the New X-Men and the Young X-Men.

Rockslide is one of the few students who kept his powers after the events of "House of M", and his friendship with Anole has been a recurring element of his stories. The character has made minor appearances in animation and video games.

== Publication history ==

Rockslide debuted in New Mutants vol. 2 #7 (December 2003) as part of the expanded student cast that DeFilippis and Weir introduced to the Xavier Institute. When the series was relaunched as New X-Men: Academy X in 2004, he became a regular supporting character and a member of the Hellions squad. He also featured in the Hellions limited series (2005), in which the squad traveled to Los Angeles and Mumbai in search of the Keller family fortune.

After Craig Kyle and Christopher Yost took over New X-Men in 2006, Rockslide was given a more central role as a member of the reduced student team, and his body was destroyed and rebuilt in the "Quest for Magik" storyline (2007). He appeared in World War Hulk: X-Men (2007) and the "Messiah Complex" crossover, and was a founding member of the Young X-Men series (2008) written by Marc Guggenheim. He subsequently appeared in X-Men: Legacy, Wolverine and the X-Men, Amazing X-Men and the Krakoa-era titles beginning in 2019. He was killed during "X of Swords" (2020) and returned in an altered form.

== Fictional character biography ==

Santo Vaccarro grows up in Boston and enrolls at the Xavier Institute after his mutation turns his body to stone. Brash and competitive, he hopes to become a professional wrestler. Emma Frost places him on the Hellions squad, where he becomes a loyal follower of team leader Julian Keller (Hellion) and develops a rivalry with the rival New Mutants squad. In the Hellions limited series, the team confronts the Kingmaker, who offers Santo a career as a wrestling champion in exchange for his future services; the squad ultimately turns against the Kingmaker.

After the Scarlet Witch strips most of the world's mutants of their powers on M-Day, Santo retains his abilities and is selected for the combined New X-Men training team. He fights William Stryker's Purifiers and the Sentinel Nimrod. In "Quest for Magik", the students are drawn into Limbo, where Santo is shattered by Belasco. He discovers that he can rebuild himself from any available stone, and his new body, formed from Limbo rock, is larger and stronger than before. During this period he befriends Victor Borkowski (Anole), supporting him after Victor loses an arm, and the two remain close afterward.

When the Hulk returns to Earth in "World War Hulk", Santo insists on challenging him for the chance to fight a legend and is quickly broken apart. In "Messiah Complex" he joins the students against the Purifiers and the Marauders, and he is later recruited by Cyclops as a founding member of the Young X-Men. He stays with the X-Men through their relocation to Utopia and then returns to the Jean Grey School under Wolverine, where he acts as a senior student and occasional instructor. He remains a reserve member of various X-Men squads in the years that follow.

In the Krakoan era, Santo lives on the mutant nation of Krakoa. During "X of Swords", he accompanies a group of young mutants to Otherworld to rescue Cypher and is killed in battle with the forces of Saturnyne. Because death in Otherworld scrambles identity, his resurrection by the Five produces a composite Rockslide whose mind holds the memories of many alternate counterparts. The restored Santo is quiet and unfamiliar, and Anole struggles to reconnect with him. He later begins to recover parts of his old personality while competing in the Krakoan fight circuit.

== Powers and abilities ==

Rockslide's body is composed of dense, granite-like rock, which gives him superhuman strength and durability and considerable resistance to physical and energy attacks. He can detach his hands and forearms and launch them as projectiles, then reattach them. Originally his form was a single rock body with living internal structure.

Following his destruction in Limbo, Santo learns that his true self is a psionic consciousness that animates rock. He can build a new body from any nearby stone, alter its size and shape, and incorporate different mineral materials. His Limbo-rock body grants him additional mass and a measure of resistance to magic. He has reformed himself after being reduced to rubble on several occasions. His Krakoan resurrection left his powers essentially unchanged, though his composite memories altered his personality.

== Other versions ==

In the limited series X-Men: The End, an adult Rockslide appears among the next generation of X-Men. In the "Age of X" reality, he is one of the mutants defending Fortress X. The composite version introduced after "X of Swords" carries the memories of numerous alternate Rockslides, several of whom are depicted in brief flashbacks.

== In other media ==

Rockslide makes a non-speaking cameo appearance in the animated series Wolverine and the X-Men. He appears as a playable character in the mobile game Marvel: Avengers Alliance and in Marvel Puzzle Quest.

== Reception ==

In 2018, CBR.com ranked Rockslide 7th in its list of the strongest students of the Xavier Institute. In 2020, Screen Rant included him among the X-Men characters most in need of a larger role.
