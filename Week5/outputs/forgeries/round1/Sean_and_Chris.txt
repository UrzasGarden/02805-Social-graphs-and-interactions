Sean and Chris Daniels are a pair of fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The twin brothers were created by Chris Matonti, J.D. Matonti and Julia Coppola and first appeared in NEW-GEN #1 (March 2010). They are the central protagonists of the NEW-GEN series, in which two teenagers raised on Earth discover that they were born on the nanotechnology-based world of New-Gen and are the sons of its creator, the scientist Gabriel.

== Publication history ==

Sean and Chris Daniels were developed by A.P.N.G. Enterprises as the lead characters of the NEW-GEN property, which was conceived as a multimedia franchise spanning comics, animation and games. The characters were created by Chris Matonti, J.D. Matonti and Julia Coppola, with artwork by Abdul H. Rashid. Marvel Comics published the series under a licensing arrangement rather than as part of its shared universe.

The brothers debuted in NEW-GEN #1 (March 2010), the first issue of a six-issue limited series that concluded later that year. A collected edition followed, and the characters returned in the sequel miniseries NEW-GEN: Forged by Fire in 2011. The series was marketed toward younger readers, and the twins were presented as audience identification figures whose discovery of New-Gen parallels the reader's introduction to the setting.

== Fictional character biography ==

Sean and Chris are born on New-Gen, a world transformed by the nanotechnology of the scientist Gabriel. When Gabriel's former partner Deadalus turns against him and seeks to control the nanites for his own purposes, Gabriel decides that his infant sons cannot safely remain on New-Gen. He sends the twins to Earth, where they are raised in a small American town without knowledge of their origins. They grow up believing themselves to be ordinary teenagers, although both display unusual aptitudes from an early age.

The two brothers have contrasting personalities. Sean is impulsive, physically confident and quick to act, while Chris is more analytical and cautious, preferring to study a situation before committing to it. Their differences are a recurring source of friction between them, but the two remain close and rely on one another when confronted with danger.

Shortly after their sixteenth birthday, agents of Deadalus locate the twins on Earth. The attack forces the brothers to flee, and they are rescued by emissaries from New-Gen, including the nanite-born Thea and the warrior Kai. Transported to New-Gen, Sean and Chris learn the truth about their father and about the conflict that has consumed the planet in their absence. Over the course of the series the brothers come to terms with their heritage and begin to develop the abilities granted to them by the nanites in their bodies.

In NEW-GEN: Forged by Fire, the twins have taken a more active role in the resistance against Deadalus. Their differing approaches to leadership are tested when Deadalus's forces mount a renewed offensive, and the brothers must learn to combine their strengths rather than compete with one another.

== Powers and abilities ==

Both brothers carry nanites inherited from their birth on New-Gen, which lie dormant during their years on Earth and activate once the twins are exposed to the planet's environment. In Sean the nanites manifest primarily as enhanced strength, speed and durability, making him a capable hand-to-hand combatant. Chris's abilities are oriented toward control and perception; he can interface with and manipulate nanite-based technology and perceive the flow of nanites around him. Neither brother has full command of his powers during the first series, and much of the narrative concerns their efforts to control them.
