Box (Roger Bochs) is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character was created by writer and artist John Byrne and first appeared in Alpha Flight #1 (August 1983). A paraplegic engineer from Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan, Bochs built and operated a robotic suit of armor called Box, and was a member of Beta Flight and later of the Canadian superhero team Alpha Flight. After his death, the Box identity passed to his colleague Madison Jeffries.

== Publication history ==

Roger Bochs was introduced by John Byrne in the first issue of Alpha Flight, where he was listed among the trainees of Beta Flight, the second tier of the Canadian government's superhuman program. Byrne had created Alpha Flight itself several years earlier in The Uncanny X-Men #120 (April 1979), but Bochs and the Box robot were new additions devised for the ongoing series. Byrne developed the character over his run on the title, during which Bochs graduated to full membership in Alpha Flight and his robot was redesigned.

Following Byrne's departure, writer Bill Mantlo took over Alpha Flight and continued the character's story. Mantlo's run emphasized Bochs's physical and mental deterioration, culminating in the "Omega" storyline, in which the character was killed in Alpha Flight #49 (August 1987). Madison Jeffries assumed the Box identity thereafter and became the version of the character most often used in later series. Bochs has made only occasional posthumous appearances, including in flashback sequences and in handbooks such as The Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe.

== Fictional character biography ==

Roger Bochs is a mechanical engineer who lost both legs and uses a wheelchair. He designs Box, a large humanoid robot that he initially pilots by remote control through a special helmet, and he is recruited by James MacDonald Hudson into Department H, where he is assigned to the training team Beta Flight. When the Canadian government withdraws funding from the program, Bochs is left without a position, although he continues to work on his robot independently.

Bochs is later approached by Jerry Jaxon, a former Department H administrator who holds a grudge against Hudson, now operating as Guardian. Jaxon recruits Bochs and several other former trainees into Omega Flight, concealing his true purpose. During Omega Flight's attack on Alpha Flight in New York, Jaxon seizes control of the Box robot from Bochs and uses it to fight Guardian. The battle ends with Guardian's apparent death and with Jaxon killed by feedback through the control helmet. Bochs, horrified at having been used, offers his services to Alpha Flight, now led by Heather Hudson, who eventually takes the name Vindicator.

Together with Madison Jeffries, a mutant with the ability to reshape metal, plastic and glass, Bochs constructs a new Box robot. Jeffries's power allows Bochs to "phase" into the machine, merging his body with the armor so that it responds as if it were his own, which gives him a mobility he lacks outside it. In this form Bochs serves as a full member of Alpha Flight, taking part in the team's battles with foes such as Pink Pearl and the Master of the World, and in the search for Sasquatch's lost spirit. He becomes romantically involved with his teammate Aurora.

Bochs's time inside the robot takes a toll on him. He finds it increasingly difficult to leave the armor, and the strain affects his judgment. When Aurora ends their relationship and turns her affections toward Jeffries, Bochs becomes despondent. Hoping to walk again, he accepts the help of Lionel Jeffries, Madison's brother, a surgeon with the mutant ability to rearrange living tissue, who grafts a new pair of legs onto him. The procedure appears successful at first, but the legs begin to reject and rot, and Bochs's mental state collapses. Lionel Jeffries, himself unstable and known as Scramble, merges with Bochs and the Box robot into a single creature calling itself Omega. Madison Jeffries, wearing a hastily assembled version of the Box armor, is forced to destroy Omega, killing both his brother and Bochs. Jeffries subsequently takes the name Box in his colleague's memory.

== Powers and abilities ==

Roger Bochs has no superhuman abilities of his own. He is a gifted engineer and robotics designer, able to construct advanced machinery with limited resources. The original Box robot grants its operator superhuman strength and resistance to injury, and is directed remotely by Bochs through a cybernetic helmet; this arrangement leaves the robot vulnerable to being commandeered by anyone else who gains access to the controls.

The second Box robot, built with Madison Jeffries, is a living-metal construct into which Bochs can phase his body. While merged with it he possesses great strength and durability, can fly by means of jets in the feet, and can perceive and manipulate electrical signals, including those of other machines. Prolonged phasing carries the risk that he will become unable to separate from the armor. Jeffries's later modifications allow the robot to alter its shape and sprout tools and weapons from its surface.

== Other versions ==

In the "Age of Apocalypse" storyline, the Box armor is worn by Madison Jeffries rather than Bochs. A version of the Box robot also appears among the technology of Department H in the Ultimate Marvel imprint, where the Canadian program is depicted as a militarized rival to the X-Men.
