Gorgon is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Jack Kirby, the character first appeared in Fantastic Four #44 (November 1965). Gorgon is a member of the Inhumans, a genetically altered offshoot of humanity, and belongs to the Inhuman Royal Family as a cousin of its ruler, Black Bolt. He is distinguished by his bull-like hooved legs, which allow him to produce powerful seismic shockwaves by stamping the ground.

The character has appeared in several animated series and video games, and was portrayed by Eme Ikwuakor in the live-action television series Inhumans (2017).

== Publication history ==

Gorgon was introduced in Fantastic Four #44 (November 1965) as the first of the Inhumans to be revealed to readers, appearing before the rest of the Royal Family was shown in the following issues. Lee and Kirby used him as an antagonist pursuing Medusa, who at that time was a member of the Frightful Four, before the Inhumans were established as a hidden civilization rather than villains.

Gorgon subsequently appeared alongside the other Inhumans in backup stories in Thor and in the first Inhumans series (1975–1977). He was a regular supporting character in the Inhumans limited series by Paul Jenkins and Jae Lee (1998–1999), and in the subsequent series Inhumans (2000) and Inhumans (2003). The character received an expanded role in Silent War (2007) and Secret Invasion: Inhumans (2008), and appeared in War of Kings (2009), Infinity (2013), Inhuman (2014–2015), Uncanny Inhumans (2015–2017), Inhumans vs. X-Men (2016–2017) and Death of the Inhumans (2018).

== Fictional character biography ==

Gorgon is born in Attilan, the hidden city of the Inhumans, as the son of Korath and a cousin of Black Bolt. Exposure to the Terrigen Mists gives him hooves in place of feet and the ability to generate tremors. He becomes the head of the Royal Family's guard and later the trainer of young Inhumans who have undergone Terrigenesis. In his first appearance he is sent by Maximus to capture Medusa and brings her back to Attilan, where the Fantastic Four encounter the Royal Family and learn of the Inhumans' existence.

Gorgon remains at Black Bolt's side through the Royal Family's conflicts with Maximus, the Kree and the United States government. In the storyline "Silent War", he is captured by the government while on a mission to recover stolen Terrigen crystals and is forcibly exposed to the Mists a second time. The second Terrigenesis transforms him into a larger, more bestial form with diminished intellect, and he is only gradually able to regain his faculties. In "Secret Invasion", Gorgon is among the Inhumans who fight the Skrulls to rescue Black Bolt, who had been replaced by an impostor. He participates in the Inhumans' conquest of the Kree Empire in "War of Kings" and later follows the Royal Family back to Earth.

During "Infinity", Gorgon defends Attilan against Thanos's forces before Black Bolt destroys the city, releasing the Terrigen Mists across the planet. He is left paralyzed from the waist down and subsequently serves as an advisor and instructor to the newly transformed Inhumans at New Attilan, led by Medusa. His mobility is eventually restored by exposure to a time-displaced version of the Terrigen Mists. In Inhumans vs. X-Men, Gorgon fights on the side of the Inhumans before the Terrigen cloud is destroyed. In Death of the Inhumans, he is killed by Vox, an agent of the Kree, during the Kree assault on the remaining Inhumans.

== Powers and abilities ==

As a result of Terrigenesis, Gorgon's legs end in hooves capable of generating seismic vibrations. By stamping the ground he can create shockwaves strong enough to shatter stone, topple structures and knock opponents unconscious. He possesses superhuman strength, durability and stamina, and his dense bone and muscle structure allows him to withstand considerable physical punishment. Following his second exposure to the Terrigen Mists, his strength and the force of his tremors increased, at the cost of a more savage temperament. Gorgon is also a skilled hand-to-hand combatant and an experienced military commander and instructor.

== Other versions ==

In the Ultimate Marvel imprint, Gorgon is reimagined as a female member of the Royal Family who first appears in Ultimate Fantastic Four Annual #1 (2005). A version of Gorgon appears in Earth X, where he remains in the service of Black Bolt. In the Marvel Zombies universe, Gorgon is among the Inhumans infected by the zombie plague.

== In other media ==

== Television ==

Gorgon appeared in the Fantastic Four animated series (1994), voiced by Michael Dorn. He appeared in Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H., Ultimate Spider-Man and Guardians of the Galaxy, voiced by Fred Tatasciore. Eme Ikwuakor portrayed Gorgon in the live-action series Inhumans (2017), which aired on ABC.

== Video games ==

Gorgon appears as a playable character in Marvel: Avengers Alliance, Marvel Future Fight and Lego Marvel Super Heroes 2.
