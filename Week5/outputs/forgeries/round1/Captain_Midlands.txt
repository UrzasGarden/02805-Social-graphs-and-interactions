Captain Midlands (Sid Ridley) is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character was created by writer Paul Cornell and artist Trevor Hairsine and first appeared in Wisdom #1 (January 2007). A British super-soldier active during the Second World War, Ridley is depicted in the present day as an elderly and bad-tempered veteran who is drawn out of retirement to serve with the intelligence agency MI-13. He is the United Kingdom's approximate counterpart to Captain America.

== Publication history ==

Captain Midlands was introduced in the six-issue limited series Wisdom (2007), published under Marvel's MAX imprint and written by Paul Cornell with art by Trevor Hairsine and Manuel Garcia. Cornell conceived the character as a deliberately unglamorous British answer to the American super-soldier archetype, giving him a regional name and a working-class background in the English Midlands rather than a national title.

The character returned in Captain Britain and MI13, also written by Cornell, with art by Leonard Kirk. He featured in the opening story arc, which served as a tie-in to the 2008 crossover "Secret Invasion", and his storyline there concluded his active role in the series. He has made no significant appearances since.

== Fictional character biography ==

Sidney Ridley is born in the English Midlands in the early twentieth century. During the Second World War, the British government selects him for a domestic attempt to replicate the super-soldier program that produced Captain America. The procedure is successful, granting Ridley enhanced physical abilities, and he is given the costumed identity of Captain Midlands. He serves on the home front and in Europe for the remainder of the war, wearing a Union Jack uniform and carrying a shield.

In the following decades, Ridley continues to operate as a government agent, but his profile remains low compared to his American equivalent. By the twenty-first century he is an octogenarian living in a residential care home, embittered by the loss of his wife and by what he regards as the country's indifference to his service. He is nonetheless still physically formidable because the serum has slowed his aging.

In Wisdom, Pete Wisdom recruits Ridley into MI-13, the agency responsible for Britain's paranormal and superhuman affairs. Ridley works alongside Wisdom, Maureen Raven, John the Skrull and Tink against a series of threats, including an incursion of faerie forces from Otherworld and a scheme involving the Martians of H. G. Wells's The War of the Worlds. He is characterised throughout by his complaining, his dislike of modern Britain and his grudging loyalty to the team.

In Captain Britain and MI13, during the Skrull invasion of Earth depicted in "Secret Invasion", Ridley is revealed to have been compromised. The Skrulls have offered to return his dead wife to him, and in exchange he has supplied them with information about MI-13 and British defences. His treachery contributes to the early losses the agency suffers when the Skrulls assault Avalon and the United Kingdom. After the invasion is repelled, Ridley is taken into custody and is last seen imprisoned, having accepted responsibility for his actions.

== Powers and abilities ==

Captain Midlands was subjected to a British variant of the super-soldier serum, which raised his strength, speed, stamina and durability to the peak of human potential. The treatment has also considerably retarded his aging, so that in his eighties he retains much of the physical capability he possessed during the war, although he is noticeably slower and more easily tired than in his youth. He is a trained soldier and an experienced hand-to-hand combatant.

In costume he carries a round shield bearing the Union Jack, which he uses both defensively and as a thrown weapon in a manner similar to Captain America, though it does not share the properties of that character's vibranium shield.
