Scarlet Spider is the name of several fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The identity is most closely associated with Ben Reilly and Kaine Parker, two genetic clones of Peter Parker, the superhero Spider-Man. The Ben Reilly version was created by writer Terry Kavanagh and artist Steven Butler and first appeared as the Scarlet Spider in Web of Spider-Man #118 (November 1994), although the clone himself had debuted in The Amazing Spider-Man #149 (October 1975). Kaine assumed the identity in Scarlet Spider vol. 2 #1 (January 2012). Other characters to use the alias include FBI agent Joe Wade, three clones of Michael Van Patrick, and, in the MC2 continuity, Felicity Hardy, the daughter of the Black Cat. The Scarlet Spider has appeared in several animated series and video games based on Spider-Man.

== Publication history ==

The Scarlet Spider identity was introduced during the "Clone Saga", a storyline that ran through the Spider-Man titles from 1994 to 1996. Ben Reilly, a clone of Peter Parker who had been presumed dead since The Amazing Spider-Man #149, returned to New York and adopted an improvised costume consisting of a red bodysuit and a sleeveless blue hooded sweatshirt. The name was coined within the story by Daily Bugle reporter Ken Ellis. The character proved popular enough to headline Web of Scarlet Spider, Scarlet Spider, Amazing Scarlet Spider and Spectacular Scarlet Spider, short-lived titles published in late 1995 when the regular Spider-Man books were temporarily renamed. Reilly set the identity aside in 1996 when he replaced Peter Parker as Spider-Man.

Joe Wade, a villainous Scarlet Spider, appeared in Web of Scarlet Spider #3–4 (January–February 1996). In Avengers: The Initiative #3 (August 2007), writer Dan Slott and artist Stefano Caselli introduced the Scarlet Spiders, a team of three clones of Michael Van Patrick outfitted with Iron Spider armor.

Kaine, who had first appeared in Web of Spider-Man #119 (December 1994), took the name in an ongoing Scarlet Spider series written by Christopher Yost with art by Ryan Stegman, launched in January 2012 and set in Houston, Texas. The series ran for 25 issues until 2014, after which Kaine joined the cast of New Warriors. Following Reilly's return in "Dead No More: The Clone Conspiracy" (2016–2017), Peter David and Mark Bagley launched Ben Reilly: Scarlet Spider, which ran for 25 issues from 2017 to 2018 and was set in Las Vegas.

== Fictional character biography ==

== Ben Reilly ==

Ben Reilly is created by the Jackal (Miles Warren) as a clone of Peter Parker. After an apparent death in battle, he survives and spends five years wandering the United States under a name taken from his uncle and aunt. When Aunt May falls ill, he returns to New York and, after fighting Venom alongside Peter, adopts the Scarlet Spider identity. He battles Kaine, the Jackal and the Grim Hunter, and briefly joins the New Warriors. When tests suggest Reilly is the original and Peter the clone, Peter retires and Ben becomes Spider-Man. He is killed by Norman Osborn in Peter Parker: Spider-Man #75 (December 1996), and his dissolving body confirms that he was the clone. The Jackal later resurrects him, and after a period as a new Jackal he resumes the Scarlet Spider identity in Las Vegas, where he struggles to reconcile his memories with his actions. He later serves as Spider-Man for the Beyond Corporation before becoming the villain Chasm.

== Kaine ==

Kaine is the Jackal's first, imperfect clone of Peter Parker, suffering from cellular degeneration that scars his face and body and grants him enhanced strength and a limited precognitive sense. He pursues Reilly across the country out of a belief that Ben is the true Peter Parker, and leaves a burning handprint known as the Mark of Kaine on his victims. During the "Spider-Island" storyline, a cure for the Spider-Virus repairs his degeneration and restores his full powers. Kaine attempts to leave New York but is stranded in Houston, where he reluctantly becomes the city's protector as a new Scarlet Spider, wearing a red and black costume given to him by Peter Parker. He later joins the New Warriors, is killed during "Spider-Verse" as the host of the Other, and returns to assist Ben Reilly in Las Vegas.

== Others ==

Joe Wade is an FBI agent who is forced by Doctor Octopus (Carolyn Trainer) to impersonate the Scarlet Spider using a holographic projector, and is defeated by Reilly and the New Warriors. The Scarlet Spiders, Michael, Van and Patrick, are clones of the Initiative recruit MVP who operate as a covert Red Team for the Initiative; Michael and Van are later killed, and Patrick leaves the program.

== Powers and abilities ==

As clones of Peter Parker, Ben Reilly and Kaine possess the superhuman strength, speed, agility and reflexes of Spider-Man, along with the ability to cling to walls and a precognitive "spider-sense". As the Scarlet Spider, Reilly adds his own inventions to the standard web-shooters, including impact webbing that bursts into a restraining net on contact, and stingers that deliver a paralyzing agent. Kaine's degeneration originally gave him greater strength and a sharper, if less reliable, form of spider-sense; after being cured he gains organic webbing, retractable stingers in his forearms and the ability to communicate with spiders.

== Other versions ==

In the MC2 universe, Felicity Hardy, the daughter of Felicia Hardy and Flash Thompson, adopts the Scarlet Spider identity without superhuman powers and attempts to partner with Spider-Girl (Mayday Parker). In the "Spider-Verse" and "Spider-Geddon" storylines, several alternate-reality Scarlet Spiders appear among the gathered spider-powered heroes, including a version of Ben Reilly who serves as the Jackal of his world.

== In other media ==

== Television ==

Ben Reilly appears as the Scarlet Spider in the Spider-Man: The Animated Series episode "Spider Wars", voiced by Christopher Daniel Barnes. A version of the character appears in Ultimate Spider-Man, voiced by Scott Porter.

== Video games ==

The Scarlet Spider costume appears as an unlockable alternate suit in Spider-Man (2000), Spider-Man: Shattered Dimensions, Spider-Man: Edge of Time and Marvel's Spider-Man (2018). Kaine's Scarlet Spider appears as a playable character in Marvel Heroes, Spider-Man Unlimited, Marvel Contest of Champions and Lego Marvel Super Heroes 2.

== Reception ==

The Scarlet Spider costume has been frequently cited as one of the more memorable alternate Spider-Man designs. In 2018, CBR.com placed Ben Reilly's hooded outfit among the best Spider-Man costumes, while Kaine's series was noted by reviewers for giving the Houston setting and its supporting cast a distinct identity from the New York-based Spider-Man titles.
