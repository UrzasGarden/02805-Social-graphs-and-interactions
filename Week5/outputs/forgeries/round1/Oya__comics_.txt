Oya (Idie Okonkwo) is a fictional superheroine appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Matt Fraction and artist Whilce Portacio, she first appeared in Uncanny X-Men #528 (November 2010). A Nigerian mutant able to raise and lower temperatures, she is one of the "Five Lights", the first new mutants to emerge after the events of "Messiah Complex" and "Second Coming". She has been a member of Generation Hope and the X-Men and a student at the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning.

== Publication history ==

Idie Okonkwo was introduced in the "Five Lights" arc of Uncanny X-Men, written by Matt Fraction with art by Whilce Portacio, as one of five mutants whose powers manifested after Hope Summers returned to the present. Her code name is taken from Oya, the Yoruba deity of winds and storms. She became a regular cast member of Generation Hope (2011–2012), written by Kieron Gillen, and was later a central character in Jason Aaron's Wolverine and the X-Men (2011–2014), where her relationships with Quentin Quire and Broo were developed. She subsequently appeared in Wolverine and the X-Men vol. 2, Amazing X-Men and various titles of the Krakoa era.

== Fictional character biography ==

Idie is a fourteen-year-old girl from a village in Oyo State, Nigeria. When her powers emerge, she accidentally freezes and burns her surroundings, and her neighbors brand her a witch and attempt to kill her. Storm and Hope Summers arrive in time to rescue her, though she has already lost her family and her home. Raised as a devout Catholic, Idie comes to believe that she is a monster and that her abilities are a mark of damnation, a conviction that shapes much of her early behavior.

On Utopia, Idie joins Hope's team, the Lights, and takes the code name Oya. In the storyline "Schism", she attends the opening of a mutant history museum in San Francisco when the new Hellfire Club takes the building hostage. With no other X-Men present, Idie kills several of the attackers to save the hostages, an act that Cyclops accepts as necessary and Wolverine regards as a child being forced to become a soldier. The dispute contributes to the split between the two men, and Idie leaves with Wolverine for the Jean Grey School in Westchester.

At the school, Idie befriends the alien student Broo and gradually begins to question her belief that she is damned. After Broo is shot during a confrontation with the Hellfire Club, she accepts an invitation from Kade Kilgore to enroll at the Hellfire Academy in order to find the person responsible. She is eventually recovered by the X-Men but is deeply affected by the episode, and she later begins a relationship with Quentin Quire. In the "Battle of the Atom" storyline, a future version of Idie is shown as a member of the X-Men. Following the establishment of the mutant nation of Krakoa, she relocates there with the other students.

== Powers and abilities ==

Oya is a mutant with the ability to manipulate temperature. She can draw heat out of one area, producing extreme cold and ice, while simultaneously releasing that energy elsewhere as intense heat or flame. Her eyes change color when she uses her powers, one turning white and the other orange. Early in her career she had little control over her abilities, and she has stated that she cannot create cold without creating heat in equal measure. She has received combat training at the Jean Grey School.

== Other versions ==

In the future depicted in "Battle of the Atom", an adult Idie is a member of the X-Men and is shown as considerably more hardened than her younger self.

== In other media ==

Oya appears as a playable character in the mobile game X-Men: Battle of the Atom.
