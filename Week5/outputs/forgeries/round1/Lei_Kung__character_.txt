Lei Kung the Thunderer is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The character was created by writer Len Wein and artist Larry Hama and first appeared in Marvel Premiere #16 (July 1974). A martial arts master of the mystical city of K'un-Lun, Lei Kung is best known as the teacher of Daniel Rand, the superhero Iron Fist, and as the father of Davos, the Steel Serpent.

== Publication history ==

Lei Kung debuted in Marvel Premiere #16 (July 1974), the second installment of the Iron Fist feature, in which he was introduced as the instructor responsible for Danny Rand's training. He remained a recurring supporting character in Iron Fist and Power Man and Iron Fist during the 1970s and 1980s. The character was given a larger role in The Immortal Iron Fist (2006–2009) by Ed Brubaker, Matt Fraction and David Aja, which expanded the history of K'un-Lun and the Seven Capital Cities of Heaven. He later appeared in Iron Fist: The Living Weapon (2014–2015) by Kaare Andrews.

== Fictional character biography ==

Lei Kung is the chief teacher of the warriors of K'un-Lun, one of the Seven Capital Cities of Heaven, which appears on Earth once every ten years. He trained Orson Randall, an earlier Iron Fist, and later took the orphaned Danny Rand as a pupil after the boy was brought to the city. Under his harsh instruction Rand becomes a skilled fighter and eventually defeats the dragon Shou-Lao the Undying to claim the power of the Iron Fist. Lei Kung's own son, Davos, fails the same trial and becomes the embittered Steel Serpent.

In The Immortal Iron Fist, Lei Kung secretly trains a group of women, among them Davos's mother Wu Ao-Shi, in defiance of K'un-Lun's rulers. During the tournament of the Seven Capital Cities, he leads a rebellion against the corrupt Yu-Ti, Nu-An, and afterward takes the title of Yu-Ti himself. He also trains Pei, a young girl from the city.

In the storyline "Avengers vs. X-Men", Lei Kung assists Iron Fist in sheltering Hope Summers in K'un-Lun, where she is prepared to face the Phoenix-empowered Cyclops. In Iron Fist: The Living Weapon, K'un-Lun is attacked by the One, and Lei Kung is killed, his severed head being delivered to Rand in New York.

== Powers and abilities ==

Lei Kung has no superhuman powers but is one of the finest martial artists in K'un-Lun, having spent decades mastering the city's fighting disciplines. He is a formidable strategist and an exacting teacher, and in some stories he wields the power of the Iron Fist himself.

== In other media ==

Lei Kung appears in the second season of the Netflix series Iron Fist, portrayed by Hoon Lee.
