Ricochet is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The identity was created by writer Tom DeFalco and artist Joe Bennett and first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #434 (May 1998), where it was one of four temporary costumed identities adopted by Peter Parker (Spider-Man). A second Ricochet, the mutant teenager Johnny Gallo, was created by Joseph Harris and Adam Pollina and debuted in Slingers #0 (December 1998). Gallo was a founding member of the Slingers and later of the support group known as the Loners.

== Publication history ==

The Ricochet persona was introduced during the 1998 Spider-Man crossover "Identity Crisis", which ran through the four monthly Spider-Man titles. In that storyline Peter Parker, unable to operate as Spider-Man, assumed four new identities, one in each book. Ricochet was the identity featured in The Amazing Spider-Man, written by Tom DeFalco with art by Joe Bennett.

Later in 1998, Marvel launched Slingers, an ongoing series by writer Joseph Harris and artist Adam Pollina that passed the four "Identity Crisis" costumes to a group of New York college students. Johnny Gallo wore the Ricochet costume. The series was cancelled with issue #12 (November 1999). Gallo subsequently appeared in Runaways (vol. 2) #1–6 (2005) by Brian K. Vaughan and Adrian Alphona as a member of the group Excelsior, and in the six-issue limited series Loners (2007) by C. B. Cebulski and Karl Moline. He has since made occasional appearances in Spider-Man-related titles.

== Fictional character biography ==

== Peter Parker ==

After Norman Osborn places a bounty on Spider-Man and frames him for murder, Peter Parker retires the identity and creates four alternative costumes with the help of Mary Jane Watson. As Ricochet, he wears a leather jacket and a mask, adopts a brash, wisecracking street-level persona, and relies on thrown metal discs along with his natural agility rather than his webbing. In the guise, he infiltrates the criminal underworld and encounters the Black Tarantula's organization before Spider-Man's name is cleared. Parker abandons the Ricochet identity, and the costumes are later obtained by the aging hero Black Marvel.

== Johnny Gallo ==

Johnny Gallo is a mutant from the Bronx whose mother was killed when he was a child. Dan Lyons, the retired Golden Age hero known as the Black Marvel, recruits Gallo and three other students, Eddie McDonough (Hornet), Cassie St. Commons (Dusk) and Ritchie Gilmore (Prodigy), and gives each of them one of Parker's former costumes. The four operate together as the Slingers. Gallo, as Ricochet, is the group's joker and the most reluctant member, and his relationship with his father is strained by his secret life. The team eventually learns that the Black Marvel has made a bargain with the demon Mephisto, and the Slingers travel to Mephisto's realm to rescue their mentor before disbanding.

Gallo later joins Excelsior, a Los Angeles support group for former teenage superheroes that includes Phil Urich, Mickey Musashi (Turbo), Chris Powell (Darkhawk), Julie Power and Chamber. The group is hired by a mysterious benefactor to bring in the Runaways, an assignment that ends in failure. The former Hornet is killed by a Hydra-controlled Wolverine during the "Enemy of the State" storyline, and Gallo is shown grieving his teammate. In Loners, the group renames itself and Gallo begins a relationship with Julie Power while struggling to keep his promise to give up costumed activity. He is one of the mutants who retain their powers after the events of "House of M".

== Powers and abilities ==

Johnny Gallo is a mutant with superhuman agility, reflexes and balance, allowing him to leap great distances and perform acrobatic feats. He also possesses a limited precognitive "danger sense" that warns him of imminent threats, similar in function to Spider-Man's spider-sense. Ricochet's primary weapons are small metal throwing discs, which he carries in his jacket and can bounce off surfaces with considerable accuracy. When Peter Parker used the identity, he relied on his own spider-derived abilities and the same throwing discs.

== In other media ==

== Video games ==

The Ricochet identity worn by Peter Parker appears as a playable character in the mobile game Spider-Man Unlimited.

The Ricochet costume is available as an alternate suit for Spider-Man in several Spider-Man video games.
