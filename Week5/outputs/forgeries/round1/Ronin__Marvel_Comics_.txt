Ronin is an alias used by several fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. The identity was created by writer Brian Michael Bendis and artist David Finch and first appeared in New Avengers #11 (November 2005). The name is taken from the Japanese term for a samurai without a master, and the costume, a dark hooded outfit with a stylized face mask, has been passed between characters rather than belonging to any one of them.

The first person to wear the costume is Maya Lopez, previously known as Echo. The identity is best known for its use by Clint Barton, who adopts it after abandoning the Hawkeye name, and it has also been worn by Alexei Shostakov (Red Guardian) and Eric Brooks (Blade). Clint Barton's version of Ronin appears in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, where he is portrayed by Jeremy Renner in Avengers: Endgame (2019) and the Disney+ series Hawkeye (2021).

== Publication history ==

Ronin was introduced in the "Ronin" storyline of New Avengers #11–13 (2005–2006) as a masked ally of the team whose identity was withheld from readers. Bendis had originally planned for the figure under the mask to be Daredevil (Matt Murdock), but the plan was changed because of developments in the Daredevil title, and the character was revealed as Maya Lopez in New Avengers #13 (January 2006). Finch designed the costume so that it concealed the wearer's gender and build, which allowed the identity to be reused.

Clint Barton took on the identity in New Avengers #27 (April 2007), following his return from death in the "House of M" and "Avengers Disassembled" storylines. He remained Ronin for the rest of Bendis's run on the title, through "Civil War", "Secret Invasion" and "Dark Reign", before returning to the Hawkeye name in 2010. Alexei Shostakov was shown using the costume in the miniseries Widowmaker (2010–2011) by Jim McCann and Duane Swierczynski. In Al Ewing's Mighty Avengers (2013–2014), a mystery character who first fought as "Spider Hero" adopted the Ronin costume and was revealed as Blade in Mighty Avengers #9 (2014).

== Fictional character biography ==

== Maya Lopez ==

Maya Lopez is recruited by Captain America and Matt Murdock to investigate the criminal organization the Hand and its ties to the Silver Samurai in Japan. Operating as Ronin so that her deafness and prior history will not be known to her enemies, she assists the New Avengers against Elektra, who has taken control of the Hand. She reveals her identity to the team and remains in Japan to continue surveillance. She is later killed by Elektra and resurrected by the Hand, but is freed from their control with the help of the New Avengers and Doctor Strange.

== Clint Barton ==

After being restored to life, Clint Barton approaches the New Avengers in the aftermath of "Civil War". Unwilling to resume his Hawkeye identity, which Kate Bishop of the Young Avengers has adopted with his blessing, he receives the Ronin costume from Maya Lopez. As Ronin he fights alongside the underground New Avengers led by Luke Cage during "Secret Invasion", in which the team battles the Skrull infiltration, and he begins a relationship with Mockingbird after she is recovered from the Skrulls.

In the storyline "Dark Reign", Barton becomes one of the most vocal opponents of Norman Osborn, publicly denouncing him on television and later attempting to assassinate him at Avengers Tower. He is captured and tortured by Osborn's forces, and the New Avengers, together with Spider-Man, rescue him. Following Osborn's defeat in "Siege", Barton puts aside the Ronin identity and returns to being Hawkeye in the series Hawkeye & Mockingbird and Avengers (vol. 4).

== Alexei Shostakov ==

In Widowmaker, a new Ronin begins killing spies associated with the Soviet-era Red Room program. Black Widow, Mockingbird and the reinstated Hawkeye pursue the killer, who is revealed to be Alexei Shostakov, Black Widow's former husband and the first Red Guardian, now working for a secret organization. He is defeated and the costume is recovered.

== Eric Brooks ==

Eric Brooks joins Luke Cage's Mighty Avengers in disguise, first in a makeshift Halloween costume as "Spider Hero" during the "Infinity" storyline and then in the Ronin costume, which he takes from the team's storage. He keeps his identity hidden because he is tracking the Deathwalkers, a cabal of immortals who have been hunting him. His identity is uncovered by the team, after which he resumes operating openly as Blade.

== Other users ==

The costume has been worn briefly by other characters, including Marc Spector (Moon Knight), who uses it as one of several alternate identities. A Skrull impersonating Ronin appears during "Secret Invasion".

== Powers and abilities ==

Ronin has no powers of its own; each wearer brings their own abilities to the identity. Maya Lopez possesses photographic reflexes that let her duplicate any physical movement she observes, which makes her a skilled martial artist and acrobat. Clint Barton is a master archer and marksman and an expert in hand-to-hand combat and swordsmanship, and as Ronin he favours a katana, nunchaku and throwing weapons rather than his bow. Shostakov is a trained Soviet operative, while Brooks has the strength, speed and healing of a dhampir. The costume itself offers light armour and conceals the wearer's identity.

== Other versions ==

In the Ultimate Marvel imprint, Clint Barton never uses the Ronin identity, but a variant of the costume appears in the series Ultimate Comics: Avengers. In the Marvel Zombies universe, a zombified Hawkeye retains his Ronin costume. The Old Man Logan continuity features an aged Hawkeye who refers to his time as Ronin.

== In other media ==

== Television ==

The Clint Barton version of Ronin appears in the animated series The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes, voiced by Chris Cox, and in Avengers Assemble, voiced by Troy Baker. Jeremy Renner's Ronin returns in the live-action series Hawkeye (2021).

== Film ==

Clint Barton adopts the Ronin identity in Avengers: Endgame (2019), where, after his family is erased by Thanos, he becomes a vigilante hunting criminals in Tokyo before rejoining the Avengers.

== Video games ==

Ronin appears as a playable character or costume in Marvel: Ultimate Alliance 2, Lego Marvel's Avengers, Marvel Future Fight, Marvel Contest of Champions and Marvel's Avengers (2020). A Ronin costume for Hawkeye is also included in Marvel Puzzle Quest.

== Reception ==

In 2019, CBR.com included Clint Barton's Ronin among its ranking of the most notable costume changes in Avengers history. Screen Rant placed the Ronin identity on its 2021 list of the best alter egos of Hawkeye.
