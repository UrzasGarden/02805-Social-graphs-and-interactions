The Tarantula is the name of several fictional characters appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Most of them have been depicted as supervillains or mercenaries, and most have been adversaries of Spider-Man. The original Tarantula, Anton Miguel Rodriguez, was created by writer Gerry Conway and artist Ross Andru and first appeared in The Amazing Spider-Man #134 (July 1974).

Later characters to use the name include Luis Alvarez, a government-sponsored operative of the fictional South American nation of Delvadia; Jacinda Rodríguez, the daughter of the original Tarantula; Maria Vasquez, a mercenary who served with Misty Knight's Heroes for Hire; and Kaine Parker, a clone of Spider-Man who was briefly transformed into a monstrous Tarantula during the "Spider-Island" storyline. The Rodriguez and Alvarez versions have each worn a spider-themed costume fitted with poison-tipped blades in the boots.

== Publication history ==

Anton Miguel Rodriguez debuted in The Amazing Spider-Man #134–135 (July–August 1974), in a two-part story by Gerry Conway and Ross Andru that also featured the second appearance of the Punisher. Conway conceived the character as a Latin American counterpart to Spider-Man, a costumed athlete whose weapons were blades rather than webbing, and placed his origin in the invented dictatorship of Delvadia. The character returned in The Amazing Spider-Man #147–148 (August–September 1975) as an ally of the Jackal during the first "Clone Saga", and was the featured villain of the opening arc of Peter Parker, the Spectacular Spider-Man #1–3 (December 1976–February 1977), written by Conway and drawn by Sal Buscema.

Writer Roger Stern and artist John Romita Jr. brought the character's story to a close in The Amazing Spider-Man #233–236 (October 1982–January 1983), a storyline that tied the Tarantula to the Brand Corporation and to Will o' the Wisp. Rodriguez was killed at the end of that arc and remained dead for more than three decades, until he was among the villains revived by the Jackal in "Dead No More: The Clone Conspiracy" (2016–2017).

A second Tarantula, Luis Alvarez, was created by writer Mark Gruenwald and artist Paul Neary and first appeared in Captain America #308 (August 1985). Alvarez was later used chiefly as a Spider-Man villain, appearing in Web of Spider-Man and The Spectacular Spider-Man in the late 1980s and early 1990s. Jacinda Rodríguez, the daughter of the original Tarantula, appeared in the Gail Simone-written series Agent X (2002–2003). Maria Vasquez was introduced by Justin Gray, Jimmy Palmiotti and Billy Tucci in Heroes for Hire vol. 2 #1 (October 2006) and remained a regular cast member for the length of that series. Kaine's transformation into a Tarantula was written by Dan Slott and illustrated by Humberto Ramos in The Amazing Spider-Man #666–673 (2011).

== Fictional character biography ==

== Anton Miguel Rodriguez ==

Anton Miguel Rodriguez is a native of the South American republic of Delvadia. As a young man he joins a revolutionary movement against the country's military dictatorship, but his comrades expel him after he needlessly kills a guard during a robbery. Rodriguez changes sides and offers his services to the government, which trains him as a costumed counter-revolutionary agent in the manner of Captain America. He proves too brutal even for the regime and flees to the United States after murdering a government official.

In New York, Rodriguez turns to piracy and extortion. In the storyline that introduces him, he and his men hijack a Hudson River cruise ship and hold its passengers for ransom, bringing him into conflict with Spider-Man. The Tarantula escapes and briefly allies himself with the Punisher, who has been tricked into believing Spider-Man is a criminal, before the two vigilantes realize the deception and the Tarantula is captured. He later works for the Jackal, who uses him to abduct Spider-Man and Ned Leeds as part of his plan to destroy Peter Parker, and for the Lightmaster, who hires him to kidnap the chairman of the Empire State University board. In that case the Tarantula is defeated by Spider-Man and the White Tiger.

Seeking real superhuman abilities, Rodriguez agrees to undergo an experimental procedure at the Brand Corporation, the Roxxon subsidiary that had earlier mutated Jackson Arvad into Will o' the Wisp. The treatment, intended to give him spider-like powers, instead begins to transform his body into that of a giant arachnid. The increasingly mindless creature rampages through the city, pursued by Spider-Man and by Will o' the Wisp, who seeks revenge on Brand. In the final moments of his humanity, Rodriguez realizes what he has become and deliberately steps into a volley of police gunfire, falling into the East River.

Many years later, the Jackal's company New U Technologies revives Rodriguez as a clone with his original memories, and he is among the restored villains who serve the Jackal in "Dead No More: The Clone Conspiracy". The cloned Tarantula continues to operate after the Jackal's downfall and later participates in the gathering of Spider-Man's enemies during "Sinister War".

== Luis Alvarez ==

Luis Alvarez is a Delvadian soldier chosen by the junta to replace Rodriguez as the state's costumed enforcer. The government subjects him to a derivative of the super-soldier process and to a series of performance-enhancing drugs, then sends him to the United States to assassinate Delvadian dissidents living in exile. His first mission brings him into conflict with Captain America and Nomad, who thwart the assassination and force him to flee. Alvarez subsequently fights Spider-Man on several occasions, once while working for the Chameleon and once alongside other hired villains, and he is defeated by Spider-Man and the Black Cat when he attempts to settle an old grievance with the wall-crawler.

After the fall of the Delvadian regime Alvarez loses his government backing and becomes a freelance mercenary, during which period he clashes with U.S. Agent. He is eventually hunted down and killed by the Jury, the armored vigilante group founded by Orwell Taylor, who regard him as a fugitive murderer.

== Jacinda Rodríguez ==

Jacinda Rodríguez is the daughter of Anton Miguel Rodriguez. Raised in exile after her father's death, she takes up his name and an updated version of his costume and weapons in order to avenge him. She is drawn into the affairs of Agency X, the mercenary firm run by the amnesiac Agent X, and comes into repeated conflict with the agency's gunslinger Outlaw. Rodríguez proves a skilled and ruthless fighter but is eventually bested, and she disappears from the agency's affairs afterwards.

== Maria Vasquez ==

Maria Vasquez is a Latina martial artist who becomes the Tarantula to avenge the murder of her sister by the Puerto Rican gangster Ricadonna. She is recruited by Misty Knight and Colleen Wing when they rebuild Heroes for Hire in the aftermath of the superhuman "Civil War", serving on a team that also includes Shang-Chi, the Black Cat, Humbug, Orka and Paladin. Vasquez is the most violent member of the group, favoring bladed weapons and showing little regard for the lives of her opponents, which repeatedly puts her at odds with Knight.

During the team's mission to the Savage Land, the Heroes for Hire attempt to rescue Moon-Boy from a group of poachers, and Vasquez is among those who face Devil Dinosaur when the creature comes to his companion's defense. In the "World War Hulk" tie-in, she is captured along with her teammates by the Hulk's Warbound and brutally tortured by the Brood-infested servants of Miek before being freed. She leaves the team after Humbug's betrayal and death, and later resurfaces as a mercenary, at one point crossing paths with Mockingbird's covert operations.

== Kaine Parker ==

Kaine, the first and most unstable clone of Peter Parker, is captured by the Jackal during "Spider-Island". The Jackal injects him with the same spider-virus that gives the population of Manhattan spider-powers, but in Kaine's degenerating body the virus produces a complete mutation, turning him into a massive, multi-limbed arachnid monster that the Jackal calls the Tarantula. The creature serves as the Jackal's and the Queen's enforcer and badly injures Spider-Man before it is subdued. At the storyline's climax, Spider-Man uses the cure developed by Mister Fantastic and Horizon Labs on the Tarantula, which restores Kaine to human form and heals his cellular degeneration. He adopts the identity of the Scarlet Spider soon afterwards.

== Powers and abilities ==

Anton Miguel Rodriguez has no superhuman powers but is an Olympic-level athlete, acrobat and hand-to-hand combatant, trained in commando tactics by the Delvadian military. His boots are fitted with retractable steel spikes coated in a fast-acting poison, and he wears gloves with similar blades. After his treatment at the Brand Corporation he gained superhuman strength and the ability to cling to surfaces, but the transformation consumed his mind within days. Luis Alvarez possessed strength and reflexes enhanced to the peak of human potential by chemical treatment, and used the same style of spiked boots and gloves as his predecessor.

Maria Vasquez is an expert in several martial arts and in the use of knives and other edged weapons; her costume incorporates blades in the forearms and boots. Jacinda Rodríguez uses weapons modelled on her father's. In his Tarantula form, Kaine had vastly enhanced strength and durability, eight limbs ending in sharp claws, and the ability to produce organic webbing and stingers; after being cured he retained many of these abilities in a controlled form.

== Other versions ==

In the Ultimate Marvel imprint, Tarantula is the name given to one of the clones of Peter Parker created by Doctor Octopus for the CIA during the "Clone Saga" storyline in Ultimate Spider-Man. The clone, who has six arms and a degenerating body, briefly battles Spider-Man before being killed when the clones' hideout is destroyed. In the Marvel Zombies universe, an undead Tarantula appears among the zombified villains who attack the surviving heroes.
