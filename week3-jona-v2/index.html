How To Play

Guess the mystery character in 6 tries using network topology clues.

After each guess, 4 tiles reveal clues:

2
Distance — BFS shortest path (hops) from your guess to the target. 🟩 = 0 (found!), 🟨 = 1, 🟧 = 2, 🟥 = 3+
⬆️
Degree Centrality — Arrow tells you if the target's degree is higher ⬆️ or lower ⬇️ than your guess.
⬇️
Betweenness Centrality — Same arrow logic for betweenness.
4
Shared Neighbors — How many neighbors your guess and the target have in common.

💡 Use centrality arrows to narrow down, and shared neighbors to triangulate!
